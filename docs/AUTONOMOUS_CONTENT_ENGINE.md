## 1. Multi-Horizon Content Planning

NovaVid 2.1 generates holistic content schedules that eliminate creator burnout and balance viral discovery against long-term channel authority.

### Strategic Dimensions:
1. **Shorts vs Long-Form Balancing:**
   - Automatically schedules YouTube Shorts on peak discovery days (e.g. Tuesday/Thursday/Monday) to feed top-of-funnel reach.
   - Schedules deep long-form videos on peak watch-time days (e.g. Saturday 12:00 PKT / Wednesday 17:00 PKT) to maximize monetization and subscriber conversion.
2. **Four Creator Strategic Objectives:**
   - **Grow Fast:** 3 long-form + 3 shorts per week; aggressive trending orientation.
   - **Build Authority:** 2 evergreen tutorials + 1 documentary breakdown per week.
   - **Monetize:** High-intent product review and tutorial balance targeting high-RPM keywords.
   - **Balanced:** 2 long-form + 1 short weekly cadence for sustainable creator operations.
3. **Weekly & Monthly Rollups:**
   - Aggregates content mix percentages.
   - Generates tactical rationale explaining why each individual slot has been chosen.
