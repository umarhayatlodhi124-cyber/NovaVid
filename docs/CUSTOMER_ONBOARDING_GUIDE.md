## 2. Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Install or Access NovaVid
- **Windows Desktop:** Run `NovaVid AI Studio_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe`. It installs instantly without requiring administrator rights.
- **Web Browser:** Visit [https://novavid-ecfc8.web.app](https://novavid-ecfc8.web.app) in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

### Step 2: Configure Your Personal AI Provider Keys (BYOA)
NovaVid utilizes a Bring-Your-Own-API model so your data belongs to you, and you never pay markup fees on AI tokens:
1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar and select **AI Providers**.
2. **Google Gemini:** Enter your free or paid Gemini API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/).
3. **Anthropic Claude (Optional):** Enter your Claude API key from [Anthropic Console](https://console.anthropic.com/).
4. **ElevenLabs (Optional):** Enter your ElevenLabs API key from [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) for neural voiceovers.
5. Click **Verify Connection**.
> [!NOTE]
> Your keys are encrypted locally inside **Windows Credential Manager** and memory-zeroized after use. They are never transmitted to LODHI TECHX servers or saved in plain text.

### Step 3: Activate Your Pilot Workstation License
1. From the sidebar, click **Plans & Licensing** (or **Customer SaaS**).
2. Enter the cryptographic license token provided by LODHI TECHX support (`NOVA_V1.<tier>...`).
3. Click **Activate License**. Your license seat is verified offline instantly, unlocking 1080p/4K rendering and professional tools.

## 3. Creating Your First Video Project

1. Click **+ New Project** in the Creator Workspace.
2. Enter your video topic (e.g. *"5 Revolutionary AI Tools Every Creator Needs in 2026"*).
3. Select your language (**English**, **Urdu**, or **Roman Urdu**).
4. Click **Generate Script** -> NovaVid automatically generates scene headings, voice narration, and visual descriptions.
5. Review the **Storyboard** and generate visual scenes using Imagen or your own imported media.
6. Click **Generate Voiceover** to synthesize narration, then review synchronized subtitles in the **Subtitle Studio**.
7. Preview your video on the **Timeline Editor**.
8. Go to **Video Export** and click **Render 1080p MP4**. Your finished video will be saved directly into your local exports folder.

---

## 4. Connecting Your YouTube Channel for 1-Click Publishing

1. Open **Google & YouTube** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Connect YouTube Account**.
3. A secure Google OAuth popup will open. Select your Google account and grant YouTube upload permissions.
4. When exporting videos, check **Auto-Schedule to YouTube** to upload your video directly with AI-optimized SEO titles, descriptions, and tags.
