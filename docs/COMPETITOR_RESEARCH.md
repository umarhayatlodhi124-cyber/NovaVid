## 1. Ethical & Permitted Competitor Intelligence

NovaVid AI Studio 2.1 allows creators to track peer channels and market competitors exclusively through permitted public YouTube endpoints.

### Parameters Analyzed:
- Channel handle and niche categorization.
- Publishing cadence (e.g. videos/week).
- Public video titles, formats, and categorization.
- Content gaps: Identifies languages, sub-topics, and modern angles that competitors consistently overlook.

### Protection of Competitor Privacy:
NovaVid strictly adheres to YouTube API Terms of Service:
- No unauthorized scraping.
- No access to private competitor analytics or revenue data.
- Recommendations focus on white-space opportunities rather than duplicate content.
