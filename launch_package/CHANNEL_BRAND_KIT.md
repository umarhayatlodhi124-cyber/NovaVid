## 3. Broadcast Standards & Audio Loudness Profile

- **Video Container:** MP4 (ISO Base Media)
- **Video Codec:** H.264 (High Profile, Level 4.2)
- **Framerate:** 60.0 fps (Constant Frame Rate)
- **Audio Codec:** AAC-LC Stereo (192 kbps, 44.1 kHz)
- **Loudness Normalization:** **-14.0 LUFS** Integrated Target, -1.5 dB True Peak (`loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11`)
- **Background Music Ducking:** Dynamic sidechain compression at -22 dB reduction during narration
