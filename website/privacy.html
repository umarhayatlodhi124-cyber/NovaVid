Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 • Published by LODHI TECHX

1. Information We Collect

NovaVid AI YouTube Studio operates as a local-first desktop application with optional cloud synchronization. We collect only data strictly necessary to provide video production and commercial licensing services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and hashed authentication credentials.
  • Hardware Machine Fingerprints: SHA-256 derived cryptographic hash of motherboard/CPU identifiers used exclusively to enforce workstation activation seat limits under your license tier. We do not store raw hardware serial numbers.
  • Usage Telemetry: Aggregated counts of monthly video exports, AI tokens consumed, and rendering hours for billing calculations.
  • YouTube Tokens: Google OAuth 2.0 refresh and access tokens are stored securely in Windows Credential Manager on your local machine and never transmitted to our marketing databases.

2. How Information is Used

We use your information solely to: provide video generation and rendering tools; authenticate commercial license keys; enforce multi-seat hardware limits; and comply with legal payment obligations through our authorized Merchant of Record.

3. Zero Data Selling Policy

LODHI TECHX does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information, scripts, generated visuals, or video content to third parties or advertising brokers.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@novavid.studio.
