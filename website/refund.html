Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 • Published by LODHI TECHX

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not completely satisfied with your commercial subscription within 14 days of your initial purchase, you are eligible for a full refund, provided your account has not consumed more than 20% of its monthly AI generation credits.

2. Subscription Cancellation & Offline Grace Period

You may cancel your recurring subscription at any time through the Customer SaaS Portal. Upon cancellation, your software remains fully active until the end of the paid billing period, followed by a 14-day server-issued offline grace period to allow you to export and back up your projects.

3. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, submit a ticket in the in-app support center or email billing@novavid.studio with your order number and registered email.
