Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NovaVid AI YouTube Studio by LODHI TECHX.

Does NovaVid AI run on 8 GB RAM?

Yes! NovaVid was engineered with strict 8 GB RAM hardening. Active idle memory is ~58 MB, and peak 4K 60fps rendering working set is only 312 MB, leaving plenty of headroom on standard creator laptops.

Do I need to pay monthly cloud rendering fees?

No. Unlike purely web-based editors that charge exorbitant cloud GPU rendering bills, NovaVid renders videos locally on your Windows PC using native FFmpeg (NVENC GPU acceleration or multi-threaded CPU).

Can I use separate Google accounts for Gemini and YouTube?

Yes! NovaVid features the Dual Independent Google Account Architecture. Your Gemini API key (existing paid account) and your YouTube channel OAuth tokens (LODHI TECHX channel) are stored in completely separate, encrypted Windows DPAPI vaults.

Does NovaVid publish videos publicly without my permission?

Never. All uploads strictly default to Private. Publishing a video publicly requires explicit interactive confirmation in the human approval modal.

What payment methods are supported?

We process payments securely via Lemon Squeezy and Paddle as Merchants of Record, supporting credit/debit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay worldwide with automated payouts to Payoneer.
