## 1. Quick Start Walkthrough

### Step 1: Launch Application & First-Run Setup
1. Launch NovaVid AI from your Windows Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.
2. If FFmpeg is not detected, NovaVid AI will present a verified one-click download dialog. Click **Install FFmpeg** to download official, SHA-256 verified binaries directly to your local application directory.
3. In **AI Providers**, click **Configure Google Gemini** and enter your API key. It is saved directly to Windows Credential Manager.

### Step 2: Produce Your First Video (Autonomous AI Mode)
1. Navigate to **AI Agent (Auto)** in the left sidebar.
2. Enter your video topic or idea (e.g. *"Top 5 AI Breakthroughs in 2026"*).
3. Select your target language (**English** or **Urdu**) and format (**16:9 Long-Form** or **9:16 Shorts**).
4. Click **Start Production Pipeline**.
5. NovaVid AI will automatically:
   - Perform competitive research and hook generation.
   - Draft a 3-act structured script (Hook, Value, Payoff).
   - Divide the script into visual scenes.
   - Synthesize neural voiceovers and generate background music with dynamic ducking.
   - Generate cinema-grade visual assets via Imagen 3.
   - Burn animated Urdu Nastaleeq or English subtitles.
   - Assemble scenes on the timeline.
6. Review the generated video at the supervised checkpoints and click **Approve & Render**.

### Step 3: Publish to YouTube
1. Go to **Publishing** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Connect YouTube Channel** to complete the 1-click Google OAuth PKCE login in your browser.
3. Review the generated SEO title, description, tags, and custom thumbnail.
4. Select privacy (**Private** or **Public**) and click **Publish Video**.
